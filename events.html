Standards & Readiness

Testing Requirements & Comprehensive Testing Preparation

Confidence comes from preparation. Follow our 4-week structured preparation protocol and review the mandatory checklist before testing day.

Testing Preparation: The 4-Week Pathway

Step-by-Step System
W1

Week 1: Stance Geometry & Form Precision

Technical Base

Instructors review proper chambering, pivot foot angles, weight distribution, and lock-in of blocks. Daily 10-minute mirror practice recommended at home.

W2

Week 2: Power Generation, Speed & Kiai

Energy & Voice

Students focus on explosive hip rotation, clean target impact on pads, and commanding vocal kiai breathing to eliminate hesitation and stage anxiety.

W3

Week 3: Mock Testing & Pressure Simulation

Live Simulation

Full trial run during regular class. Evaluators provide live scoring on forms, controlled sparring combinations, and board-breaking alignment with instant coaching tips.

W4

Week 4: Mental Focus, Rest & Uniform Etiquette

Mindset & Ceremony

Light tapering drills, uniform cleaning, belt knot tying perfection, and positive mental visualization exercises to arrive calm, confident, and ready to shine.

Dojo Prep Culture

"We do not rise to the level of our hopes, we fall to the level of our training."

Testing Requirements

Strict Eligibility Rubric
85%+ Attendance Consistency

Minimum 24 classes attended during the current ranking cycle without unexcused extended lapses.

3 Skill Stripes Earned

Red (Technique), Blue (Fitness), and White (Character/Discipline) stripes affixed to current belt.

Parent & Teacher Character Slip

Signed confirmation verifying good manners, room tidiness, and positive effort at home and school.

Lead Instructor Nomination

Personal invitation slip issued by Head Instructor Sensei Marcus verifying readiness to test.

The Grand Gala

Honoring Excellence in Achievement Ceremonies

The culmination of sweat, dedication, and growth. Families gather in our ceremonial arena as newly promoted champions are awarded their new belts, medals, and official certificates.

01

The Traditional Bowing-In & Sensei Oath

The ceremony opens with formal bowing to parents and instructors, honoring the family support system that made each child's martial journey possible.

02

The Untying & Tying of the New Rank

Head Sensei gently unties the old belt and binds the new color around the student's waist, symbolizing the shed of past limitations and entry into higher responsibility.

03

Official World Federation Certification

Each student is presented with an embossed certification certificate featuring their rank, dojo seal, and evaluator master signatures suitable for framing.

04

The Unified Grand Kiai & Family Photo Salon

All graduating warriors execute a simultaneous victory kiai that rattles the arena rafters, followed by professional studio photos with parents and coaches.
