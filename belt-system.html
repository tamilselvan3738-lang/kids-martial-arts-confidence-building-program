Promotion Criteria

The 5-Gate Promotion Covenant: Exactly How Belt Eligibility Is Earned

To eliminate anxiety and ensure absolute transparency for parents, every promotion requires passing through 5 unambiguous gates. If any gate is pending, Sensei provides friendly targeted coaching.

1

Gate 1: Minimum Mat Hours Validated

Verified

Student has achieved the required number of verified classes (e.g. 24–30 classes for Kyu ranks) with active participation.

2

Gate 2: All 3 Skill Stripes Earned

Required

The student has received their White, Red, and Gold stripes physically pinned to their existing belt by an instructor.

3

Gate 3: Pre-Test Technical Clearance

Instructor Sign-Off

The child executes their Kata and Kihon without teacher prompting during an informal mock evaluation two weeks prior.

4

Gate 4: Home & School Character Intent Card

Parent Signed

Parents confirm that the student demonstrates respect, completes chores, and finishes homework at home before promotion is granted.

5

Gate 5: Formal Promotion Invitation Ticket

Golden Invitation

Head Sensei personally hands the child their golden promotion invitation in front of their class peers with a ceremonial bow.

Progression Guidelines

The Sacred Belt Knotting Tradition

The square knot sits directly over the Dan-Tian (center of gravity), symbolizing balanced emotions, centered focus, and humility.

We maintain a strictly supportive environment. Children are never publicly "failed" or shamed. If a movement is incomplete, Sensei kneels beside them, performs a gentle reset breathing exercise, and gives them a second private opportunity. If more practice is required, they receive targeted one-on-one tutoring without additional exam fees.
In our school, skipping belts is strictly prohibited. Even naturally gifted athletes require emotional maturation, patience, and humility. Faster students are instead invited to our Junior Leadership Squad to mentor newer white belts, which cultivates empathy over arrogance.
Our minimum age for Junior Black Belt (1st Poom) is 10 years old with at least 3 consecutive years of disciplined training. Students under 10 who complete the curriculum are awarded Senior Red/Black Apprentice rank until they reach the required emotional and physical maturity.
