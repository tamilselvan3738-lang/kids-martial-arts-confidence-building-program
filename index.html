Dojo Kun: The 4 Honor Milestones
Character Architecture

Discipline, Respect & Leadership Development

In Japanese tradition, martial arts begins with courtesy and ends with respect. We don't train kids to be fighters; we train them to become respectful leaders and community role models.

1

The "Bow-In" Ritual

Teaching humility, recognizing coaches and classmates, and resetting the mind before training.

2

Courage Over Comfort

Embracing difficult physical drills and speaking up firmly when someone is being bullied.

3

Indomitable Spirit

Seven times down, eight times up. Reframing initial failure as essential fuel for growth.

4

Junior Leadership Track

Senior belts assist junior ranks, lead warm-ups, and embody positive peer role modeling.
