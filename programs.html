Program Overview • The Complete Belt Progression Journey

An 8-Stage Ladder of Character, Courage & Unshakable Self-Worth.

At our Academy, our martial arts curriculum is divided into progressive rank phases meticulously calibrated for child development psychology. We do not rush children through meaningless fast-track testing; each belt stage is a celebrated milestone that instills lifelong motor agility, respectful boundaries, anti-bullying armor, and executive focus.

Little Warriors • Early Childhood Foundations (Ages 3 to 5)

Where Tiny Ninjas Build Giant Confidence

Designed specifically for preschool minds and bodies. We transform raw energy into bilateral coordination, listening compliance, and safe falling reflexes through gamified movement.

Ages 3 to 5 Curriculum

The Listening Freeze Principle

Children master rapid compliance through joyful games: freeze instantly when Sensei calls “Kiai!”

Pillar 01

Beginner Movement Skills

Animal-based movement conditioning: Bear Crawls for scapular stability, Frog Jumps for explosive lower-body power, and Turtle Rolls for safe head-tucked falling mechanics.

✓ Safe Tumbling ✓ Spatial Boundaries ✓ Core Trunk Strength
Pillar 02

Balance & Coordination

Single-leg balancing on soft sensory domes, hand-eye target tapping, and vestibular balance obstacle courses that dramatically accelerate gross motor milestones.

✓ Single-Leg Flamingo Stand ✓ Bilateral Motor Drills ✓ Agility Stepping Stones
Pillar 03

Basic Discipline Activities

Building polite manners, holding eye contact with coaches, practicing the traditional bowing oath, and mastering the habit of placing shoes and water bottles neatly in dojo cubbies.

✓ 3-Second Listening Freeze ✓ Yes Sensei Courtesy ✓ Cubby Tidy Habit
Novice Cohorts • White & Yellow Belts (Ages 6 to 8)

From the Clean White Canvas to the First Sunbeam

Where martial fundamentals, proper stances, crisp defensive blocks, and radiant vocal confidence take root.

Precision in Posture: Zenkutsu-Dachi & Age-Uke

Building bilateral core power, balance, and unwavering focus on the mat

Foundational Ranks
Phase 01

White Belt Program

The Clean Canvas

Every master began here. The White Belt curriculum introduces students to ancient martial traditions, posture discipline, and foundational self-defense safety blocks.

Martial Arts Fundamentals

Dojo etiquette, uniform folding, belt knotting, and traditional Rei bowing ceremonies.

Basic Stances

Front Stance (Zenkutsu-dachi) and Horse-Riding Stance (Kiba-dachi) for rooted stability.

Basic Blocks

High Block (Age-uke) and Downward Sweeping Block (Gedan-barai) to deflect incoming forces.

Beginner Fitness

Agility ladder runs, core sit-up holds, safe break-falls, and foundational stamina.

Phase 02

Yellow Belt Program

First Rays of Sun

The seed has sprouted. Yellow Belts combine stances with sharp offensive strikes, master target focus, and unlock vocal confidence through vocal Kiai power.

Fundamental Techniques

Front Snap Kick (Mae-geri), Straight Punch (Choku-tsuki), and palm heel self-defense strikes.

Movement Combinations

Step-and-strike sequences, high-block into reverse punch, and multi-directional shifts.

Focus Training

Single-point target focus on focus pads, maintaining eye contact, and silencing distraction.

Confidence Exercises

Loud resonant Kiai shouting drills that eliminate social timidity and public speaking fear.

Intermediate Ranks • Orange & Green Belts (Ages 8 to 10)

Precision, Controlled Sparring & Rising Leadership

Students advance from static forms to dynamic sparring reactions, distance control, personal discipline, and measurable goal setting.

Orange Belt Program

The Fiery Spark

Intermediate Techniques

Roundhouse kick (Mawashi-geri), knife-hand strike (Shuto-uchi), and back stance balance.

Controlled Sparring Skills

Distance management, zero-contact touch tag drills, and soft protective foam gear sparring.

Reaction Training

Split-second visual pad reactions and fluid duck-and-counter defensive head movement.

Personal Discipline

Tracking daily homework independently, packing own sparring bag, and morning routine grit.

Controlled Precision
Safety-First Sparring Standards
Reaction Reflex Drill 0.24s (Sensei Speed)

Green Belt Program

The Sprouting Tree

Advanced Fundamentals

Side Piercing Kick (Yoko-geri), Backfist Strike (Uraken), and dynamic jumping evasions.

Technique Combinations

5-part continuous fluid counter katas (Heian Nidan / Taeguk Il-Jang) with power breathing.

Leadership Activities

Leading class warm-up jumping jacks, counting in traditional Japanese, and greeting novices.

Goal Setting

Quarterly stripe milestone tracking sheets and personal confidence goal charting.

Advanced Senior Cohorts • Blue & Brown Belts (Ages 10 to 13)

Tactical Strategy, Mentorship & High-Level Refinement

Where youth transition from students into mentors — refining micro-mechanics, executing complex strategies, and guiding younger belts with patient empathy.

Senior Peer Mentorship In Action

To Teach is to Learn Twice

By assisting Little Warriors and White Belts, senior youth develop profound patience and servant leadership.

Blue Belt Program

The Vast Horizon

Advanced Martial Arts Skills

Jump front kicks (Tobi Mae-geri), hook kicks, and dynamic swept evasions.

Strategy Development

Ring awareness, baiting counter-strikes, and defensive angle cutoff timing.

Mentoring Skills

Assigned junior buddy coaching during forms and partner footwork drills.

Mental Strength Training

Breathing through intense physical fatigue and stress visualization drills.

Brown Belt Program

The Grounded Mountain

Advanced Technique Refinement

Micro-adjustments in hip torque, joint lock mechanics, and kata bunkai applications.

Leadership Responsibilities

Serving as floor captain, supervising bow-in rituals, and modeling dojo etiquette.

Teaching Assistance

Holding kicking targets for beginners and coaching Little Warriors through warmups.

Performance Preparation

Demonstration team synchronized katas, pine board breaking, and Dan prep clinics.

Junior Black Belt Dan Degree

The Black Belt Mindset

“A black belt is not something you wear; it is someone you become.”

Black Belt Journey • The Shodan Summit

The Black Belt Journey: Character Forged in Iron

Earning an authentic Black Belt takes 3+ years of rigorous dedication. It represents the transformation of a child into an inspiring, humble leader who commands respect wherever they walk.

1

Black Belt Requirements

Mastery of 8 traditional katas, 1,000-pushup cumulative stamina test, and academic school report submission (B average required).

2

Long-Term Goal Planning

Multi-year timeline architecture: teaching children how to break 36-month lofty ambitions into daily 1% compounding habits.

3

Leadership Development

Certified Junior Sensei credential, teaching full 45-minute junior clinics, and delivering an oral thesis on martial virtues.

4

Advanced Training

Auditory blindfold sparring evasion, multiple attacker de-escalation drills, and advanced tactical joint lock releases.

5

Black Belt Preparation

6-month intensive candidate boot camp, breaking 3 solid pine boards, and reading the Black Belt Honor Oath to family.
