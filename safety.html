Child Safety Guidelines

Uncompromising Protection & Dojo Safety Culture

Your child’s physical security and emotional well-being are our highest sacred duty. Discover our comprehensive Child Safety Guidelines and strict Training Safety Rules engineered by pediatric martial arts specialists.

01 Physical Protection & High-Density Tatami Mats

Our entire training facility is floored with 2-inch professional shock-absorbing closed-cell tatami foam mats. All wall pillars and mirrors are wrapped in 6-foot heavy foam padding to prevent impact trauma.

02 Emotional Encouragement & Bullying Prevention

Child Safety Guidelines extend beyond bruises. We cultivate an inclusive brotherhood and sisterhood where sarcasm, mockery, or aggressive intimidation are strictly forbidden and immediately resolved with restorative dialogue.

03 Certified Staff & Two-Adult Rule Compliance

Every instructor undergoes comprehensive background checks, pediatric CPR/AED certification, and SafeSport misconduct training. We maintain a strict "Two-Adult Rule" guaranteeing no child is ever left alone with a single adult in private.

Dojo Protocol

Training Safety Rules

Mandatory Code of Dojo Conduct

1. Mat Bowing & Attention

Students bow upon entering and leaving the mat to center their minds, signaling total readiness to listen and follow instructor commands.

2. Universal "Maté" Freeze

When an instructor shouts "Maté" (Stop!) or blows the safety whistle, all movement, strikes, and sparring cease instantly without question.

3. Personal Gear Check

Fingernails and toenails must be trimmed short. No watches, rings, jewelry, or hard hairpins are permitted during practice to prevent scratches.

4. Safe Spatial Bubbles

Students maintain an arm's-length safety perimeter during kicking combinations. Running or unsupervised wrestling is strictly prohibited.

Pediatric Pedagogy

Attentive Instructor-to-Student Guidance & Safe Training Practices

Safety starts with skilled, watchful mentorship. Our low student-to-teacher ratio allows coaches to guide every kick, breakfall roll, and movement with personalized biomechanical oversight.

1:6 Ratio
Eye-Level Coaching & Positive Reinforcement

Instructor-to-Student Guidance Protocols

1. Eye-Level Kinetic Communication

Instructors crouch to the child's height to eliminate intimidation, offering patient visual demonstrations and clear, age-appropriate verbal cues before any contact begins.

2. Respectful Boundary & Target-First Spotting

Corrections are delivered by positioning target shields, foam noodles, and floor markers rather than unnecessary physical touching, honoring each child's bodily boundaries.

3. Neurodivergent & Sensory-Friendly Pacing

Our faculty is trained in pediatric sensory regulation. If a child feels overwhelmed or overstimulated, they are offered quiet mat breaks without penalty or shame.

Parental Alliance

Constructive Parent Participation Guidelines

Parents are our indispensable co-pilots in building resilient young warriors. We have established clear Parent Participation Guidelines to create an inspiring, pressure-free atmosphere for all children.

Celebrate Effort Over Winning

Praise your child for standing tall after a stumble, attending class on days they feel tired, and trying difficult combinations. Character is built in the effort, not just the medal.

The No-Sideline-Coaching Rule

To prevent split attention and confusion for your child, kindly refrain from shouting instructions from the viewing lounge. Allow the instructors to be the single voice of direction during mat time.

100% Glass-Partition Transparency

You are welcome to observe every class from our dedicated viewing pavilion. We never hold private or closed-door sessions away from parental view.

Open Sensei Communication Hours

Have questions about your child's progress or behavioral goals? Instructors are available 15 minutes before and after each class block for parent dialogue.

Lounge Amenities for Parents

Enjoy high-speed optical Wi-Fi, organic herbal tea, charging stations, and comfortable ergonomic seating while watching your child conquer new milestones.

Perimeter Security

Strict Attendance & Pickup Policy & Guardian Release Gate

We operate an airline-grade check-in and pickup security protocol. No child ever exits our facility without a matched, pre-authorized adult guardian.

Pediatric Wellness

Holistic Health & Fitness Considerations

Every child’s physiology is unique. We tailor training intensity, hydration intervals, and medical accommodations to support each young warrior’s healthy physical development.

Hydrate & Recover
Structured 15-Minute Hydration Breaks

Asthma & Allergy Action Plans

Emergency inhalers and EpiPens are tagged in our front-office medical cabinet. Coaches know individual triggers before class starts.

Hydration & Thermoregulation

Mandatory water breaks are built into every 15-minute training cycle. Reverse-osmosis filtered water is free for all students.

Growth-Plate Safe Conditioning

No heavy weights or spinal axial loads. All youth strength development utilizes bodyweight calisthenics and plyometrics.

Graduated Return-to-Mat Policy

Students recovering from illness, fractures, or concussions must provide physician clearance before resuming physical contact.

Rapid Response

Ready Action Emergency Procedures & Parent Safety Resources

Preparedness brings peace of mind. Review our 4-tier Emergency Action Plan and access downloadable safety resources for your family.

Emergency Procedures Action Plan

Response Speed: <30 Seconds
L1

Minor Tumble & First-Aid Response

Cold compression applied immediately for mild sprains or bumps. Student is escorted to comfortable rest bench and parent is sent an immediate SMS update.

L2

Automated External Defibrillator (AED) on Site

Wall-mounted ZOLL Pediatric-capable AED unit located at mat entry, inspected weekly. All full-time coaches certified bi-annually in pediatric CPR.

L3

Direct EMS Paramedic Integration

Direct emergency dispatch hotkey connects directly to the local fire department and paramedic station located only 1.2 miles from our academy doors.

L4

Dual Emergency Evacuation Corridors

Direct-to-outside panic push-bar exits at both ends of the dojo. Students participate in seasonal calm evacuation drills to ensure zero panic during alarms.
